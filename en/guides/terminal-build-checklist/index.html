Before you commission a trading terminal

25 questions to ask before signing a terminal contract — yourself, your vendor and a white-label provider. A 3-page PDF, no sign-up.

Download PDFDiscuss a project

PDF, 3 pages, 83 KB, no sign-up

A guide for founders and product owners who are about to commission a trading terminal or compare building it against a white-label platform. It is not about the trendiest stack, but about the decisions that set your budget and timeline six months from now.

Inside: 25 questions across five blocks — product, data and realtime, money and security, team, operations. We collected them from discovery work and audits: most expensive rewrites start with a question nobody asked in time.

The checklist is free and requires no email. Download the PDF and work through it for your own project — 15–20 minutes. If more than five answers are “don't know”, start with discovery or an audit rather than development.

CONTENTS

What is inside

Five blocks of five questions each — from user scenarios to cost of ownership.

01

Product and scenarios

Who the terminal is for, which scenarios must ship in v1, and what is deliberately out of scope.

02

Data and realtime

Market-data sources, history, disconnects, event ordering, latency and degradation when volatility spikes.

03

Money and security

Licences and permissions, trading integrations, payments, keys and access, storage of sensitive data.

04

Team and process

Who writes the code and who maintains it, demos and scope transparency, repository and infrastructure handover.

05

Operations

Data-stream monitoring, alerts, load testing, updates and the cost of ownership a year from now.

PROCESS

How to use it

01

Work through the checklist

Download the PDF and answer the questions for your project or a vendor proposal. 15–20 minutes.

02

Mark the red flags

Unanswered questions and “don't know” are budget risks. List them separately — they become the discovery scope.

03

Book a diagnostic call

We go through the red flags in a 30-minute call and tell you what to fix first and what can wait.
