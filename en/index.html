FAQ

Questions we get before the start

How much does a project cost?
Discovery from $2,500, MVP from $12,000, trading terminal from $25,000, web3/dApp from $8,000, web services from $6,000. The exact price comes after discovery, against a concrete scope.
Can we start without discovery?
Yes, if the job is an audit or a targeted improvement. For a new product discovery pays for itself: without it an estimate turns into guesswork and the scope becomes endless.
Do you work with trading systems and real money?
Yes, with one caveat: licences, exchange permissions and legal matters are on the client. We own the code, architecture and system resilience.
Who owns the code?
The client. Repository, infrastructure and documentation are handed over at project transfer. We do not hold access through private dependencies.
How do you work remotely?
A distributed team in UTC+3, with scheduled calls and demos. For dedicated teams we adapt to your process.
What will you not take on?
“$300 websites”, ponzi schemes, “guaranteed signals”, KYC circumvention and grey schemes. We also do not fix a whole-project price without discovery.

Tell us what you are building

A 30-minute diagnostic: we look at the task, risks and the next step. No obligations and no 40-slide deck.
