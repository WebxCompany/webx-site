05 / TEAM

A dedicated product team

When a product is alive but the tasks no longer fit in the founder's head, what you need is not “an outsourced team” but a rhythm: backlog, sprint, demo, retro. We bring that rhythm and people who keep the context between sprints.

Discuss the taskPackages and pricing

What we do

  • Dedicated team: PM, two developers, QA, designer when needed
  • Two-week sprints with planning, demo and retro
  • Transparent backlog and hour-based estimates
  • Weekly status and work report
  • Onboarding into your process or our operating frame
  • Team composition and timelines are fixed in the contract: the team is assembled per project

Who it is for

  • Products with users but without a stable engineering team
  • Companies that need to cover a direction (trading, web3, fintech)
  • Teams where delivery is stuck for lack of people
  • Projects that need predictable feature flow, not heroic sprints

Scope of work

  • Team composition and roles for your stack
  • Joint quarterly and sprint planning
  • Development, review, testing, releases
  • Quality metrics: defects, lead time, critical-path coverage
  • Monthly review and backlog adjustment

FAQ

Common questions

Can we take just one developer?
A dedicated team starts with PM + two developers + QA so we keep speed and quality. One developer is project work with a different scope, not a squad.
How do you report?
A weekly status: done, in progress, risks. A demo every two weeks. Hour-based reporting under the T&M model.
Can you work in our Jira/GitHub?
Yes. We onboard into your tools and keep your process. If there is none, we propose a minimal working rhythm and set it up.
